BRAND SHEET

The sign over the door of the people a hero walks past.

The emblem is a tavern signboard. Where a hero's crest would carry crossed swords, ours carries a hammer and a chef's knife, the tools of the trades. The tankard is the innkeeper's, and above it hangs the marker every adventurer reads as this one has work for me. You are the NPC. You run the village, and you are the one who knows.

Warm, comic and a little put-upon, until someone finds the blacksmith in the river.

Logo

Lettering is Cinzel Extra Bold, converted to outlines. The ribbon is the label on the box.

Palette

Oak, iron, bone and ember are the game's own colours from Ui/Theme.cs, so the logo and the chrome are one family. The last line on each swatch is ready to paste into C#.

Using it

Ember is rare

One ember thing per screen: the quest marker, the call to action, a lit window. If everything glows, nothing is hiring.

Dark ground, bone text

The brand lives in the dark of the common room. Use Oak Deep or Cellar as the page and Bone for text, Parchment for the quieter lines.

Clear space and size

Keep the height of the quest marker (about one fifth of the emblem) clear on every side. Full-colour emblem down to 32 px; below that, draw a simplified icon.

Never

Put a hero on it. No swords, shields, helmets or dragons: the brand is the world around them. Don't recolour the ribbon, stretch the lettering, or put the emblem on a busy photo without the ink version.
