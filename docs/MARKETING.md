# Marketing

How the game finds its players, and when. The goal is a game we love playing with friends and
family; anything past that is a bonus. Every step here is cheap, and none of it needs the game to
be a hit to be worth doing. The build-side timing (store page, Steamworks, Early Access) lives in
[ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md), "Toward Steam"; this file is the people side. The store page draft is
[STEAM-PAGE.md](STEAM-PAGE.md); setting up the accounts is [SOCIAL.md](SOCIAL.md). Voice and words: the brand guide (`art/brand/brand-guide.html`).

**The dates are now set by Next Fest (22 Feb to 1 Mar 2027)**, and the week-by-week plan is
[NEXT-FEST.md](NEXT-FEST.md). That puts the phases below side by side, not one after another:
- Phase 1 runs through the holidays.
- Phase 2 (the page) is done by mid-December.
- Phase 3 starts the day the page goes up.
- Phase 4 is February. Early Access follows in spring or summer, with traitors.

## The principle

In a multiplayer game, every player is a marketer: each one brings a group. So the cheapest
marketing is making it easy to install, host and join, and making rounds that people retell
afterwards. Paid ads come last, if ever.

## Phase 1: friends and family (now)

The question isn't reach. It's whether the game is fun with people who aren't us.

- [ ] Get the Discord onto the playtest build and playing regular rounds.
- [ ] **Watch for coming back unprompted.** Someone asking "are we playing Friday?" is the real
      signal; polite praise isn't.
- [ ] **Collect the stories.** After a round, write down what people retold ("the inn caught fire
      while Dad argued with the musician about prices"). These become the trailer, the screenshots
      and the pitch. F11 notes and `logs/rounds/` help; keep a running list below.
- [ ] **Record play.** OBS running during playtests. Clip the funny moments, even roughly.
- [ ] **Smooth the join path.** Note every place someone got stuck installing, hosting or joining,
      and fix those before anything else. Friction there costs more than marketing can win back.

*Move on when:* friends of friends, who don't know us, play it and ask to play again. For the
fest we can't wait for that: keep doing Phase 1 alongside the rest through the holidays.

## Phase 2: the store page (public by 18 Dec 2026)

See NEXT-FEST.md for the dates and ROADMAP.md for what the page needs built. On the people side:

- [ ] Check the name: search Steam, itch and the trademark databases (USPTO, EUIPO) for
      "Heroes Not Included" and near matches. The nod to *Oxygen Not Included* is fine as long as
      nothing is too close.
- [ ] Capsule art: it matters more than anything else on the page. Brand colours, the emblem, a
      busy common room at dusk, no heroes in front. Made in-house for the fest. Commission an
      artist's version after it, before Early Access.
- [ ] Screenshots now. A 30 to 60 second trailer cut from real play, by late January.
- [ ] Put the page up as "Coming soon" and point the Discord at it: wishlists are what Steam reads
      at launch.
- [ ] Steam Playtest in place of the itch page, optional, and not during the fest.

## Phase 3: growing wishlists (from the page going up, mid-December, to launch)

- [ ] **Short clips, often.** 15 to 30 seconds, one moment each, on YouTube Shorts, TikTok and
      Reddit (r/IndieDev, r/indiegames, r/IndieGaming, r/CozyGamers; follow each sub's
      self-promotion rules). Lead with the hook: you're the shopkeepers the heroes walk past.
- [ ] **A devlog cadence** we can keep: one post or clip a week beats a burst then silence. The
      Discord and the Steam page's news get the same post.
- [ ] **Small creators.** Streamers and YouTubers with 1k to 50k followers who play chaotic co-op
      or social deduction with a regular friend group. Offer keys for their whole group. Small
      creators reply; big ones find you through small ones.
- [ ] **Traitors are the streamer hook.** Social deduction is what spreads on stream. Push hard
      only once Whispers (M4) plays. That's after the fest: the demo's hook for creators is
      chaotic co-op, a town on fire with friends behind the bar.
- [ ] Press kit page: logo, emblem, screenshots, the trailer, a paragraph, contact. Free on itch.
      Ready by late January, for the Press Preview.

## Phase 4: Next Fest (22 Feb to 1 Mar 2027) and launch

**Next Fest** is the biggest free visibility Steam gives, and a game only gets one. Ours is
February 2027 (dates and deadlines: [NEXT-FEST.md](NEXT-FEST.md)).

- [ ] **The demo:** one Millbrook season, shortened to 20 to 25 minutes, with six trades (the
      innkeeper, cook, smith, empath, tinker and carpenter). It's solo-first, with bots filling the
      empty trades, and friends can join through a Steam invite. No traitors. The practice yard
      and its lessons come too.
- [ ] **Register by 6 Jan** (it closes the 10th).
- [ ] **The demo public by about 8 Feb**, two weeks early:
  - Press can play it at the Press Preview (11 Feb).
  - Steam lets us email every wishlister about a new demo, within 14 days of its release.
  - There's time to fix what the first strangers find.
- [ ] **Creators:** demo access to the 30 to 50 small creators on the list in early February,
      with an offer of keys for their whole group. Clip their rounds, with permission.
- [ ] **During the fest:**
  - Our own streams with the Discord, as many days as we can keep up.
  - Replies on the Steam discussions every day.
  - A fix build every other day at most.
- [ ] **After the fest:**
  - Read the recap: wishlists, demo players, median playtime.
  - Email the creators who played, again a week or two before launch.
- [ ] **Early Access launch in spring or summer 2027, with traitors**, and the Discord as home
      base. Launch is the one big moment, and launching thin costs more than launching a few
      months later.

Rough numbers people quote: a few thousand wishlists at launch is a decent start, and ~7 to 10k
tends to get Steam's own recommendations working for you. Fewer is fine, just quieter.

## Don't

- Pay for ads, followers or "marketing services" that email you. They rarely pay for a small game.
- Name the games that inspired us on the page or in the art (CLAUDE.md, Rules). Comparing in
  conversation ("like Among Us, but you run the tavern") is fine.
- Show strangers the game before the join path is smooth. First impressions only happen once.
  The fest brings strangers in February regardless, so that's the deadline for a smooth join path.
  For most of them the join path is a solo demo: the tutorial, and a first round they understand.
